02 / What happens next

From deposit to paid call

01

Deposit

You approve USDT and deposit it into the vault. The balance is recorded against your address.

deposit(10.00 USDT)
02

Call a priced route

Your agent requests a paid endpoint and receives HTTP 402 with a price and an invoice id.

402 PAYMENT REQUIRED
03

Sign a voucher

The agent signs EIP-712 locally. No transaction, no gas, no chain write from the agent.

SIGN_TYPED_DATA
04

Settle

The facilitator submits the voucher and returns the data with an on-chain receipt.
