200 + X-PAYMENT-RESPONSE
Why this scheme

USDT changed the architecture.

Standard x402 exact settlement expects EIP-3009. BOT Chain USDT does not expose it, so AgentPay uses a prepaid vault and signed vouchers instead of pretending the token supports something it does not.
